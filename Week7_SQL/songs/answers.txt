Question 1: If songs.db contains the top 100 songs of one listener from 2018, how would you characterize their audio aura?
By averaging the energy, valence, and danceability values of the top 100 songs, we can describe their aura.
For example:

    High energy (e.g., 0.75): lively and intense.
    Moderate valence (e.g., 0.6): somewhat positive mood.
    High danceability (e.g., 0.8): suitable for dancing.
    This suggests an energetic and upbeat aura.

Question 2: Why might this method not be very representative, and how could it be improved?
Limitations:

    Treats all songs equally, ignoring play frequency.
    Ignores temporal or contextual differences (e.g., mood changes).

Improvements:

    Weight songs by play count.
    Analyze by time periods (e.g., seasons).
    Exclude outliers that skew results.
